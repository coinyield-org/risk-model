## Overview

M0 is an institutional stablecoin infrastructure protocol that enables approved
institutional minters to issue M tokens against US Treasury backing, and approved
validators to earn yield from the protocol. M is designed as a wholesale stablecoin
infrastructure layer, not a retail product — participation is KYC-gated and
institutionally restricted. Backing consists of off-chain US Treasuries held by approved
minters; the protocol uses Certora formal verification rather than traditional PDF audits.
M0's primary differentiation from USDC/USDT is its permissioned multi-issuer model
where minter risk is distributed rather than concentrated.
